Privacy Policy

Publisher: GTM Development Johansen, Maristuen 3, 9011 Tromsø, Norway Contact: andre.aa.johansen@gtmfm.no Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy covers the Atlassian Cloud apps published by GTM Development Johansen ("we", "us"), starting with Sprint Scope Tracker for Jira, and this website.

1. Summary

  • Our apps are built on Atlassian Forge and run entirely inside Atlassian's cloud.
  • No personal data leaves Atlassian. Our apps make no calls to servers we or anyone else operate.
  • App data is kept in Forge storage, which Atlassian hosts and isolates per customer site.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data, and we do not use it for advertising or AI training.

2. What the apps process

Data Where it comes from Why Where it is kept
Sprint IDs, names, states and dates Jira Software To know when a sprint started and closed Forge storage on your site
Issue IDs and the time an issue was added to or removed from a sprint Jira issue history To show scope changes and answer the JQL functions Forge storage on your site
Atlassian account ID of the person who made the change Jira issue history To show who changed scope Forge storage on your site

Issue keys, summaries, statuses and people's display names and avatars are not stored. Jira shows them live, and only to people who are allowed to see them in Jira.

The apps do not read issue descriptions, comments or attachments, and they do not collect email addresses.

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

When you, the customer, use the app, you are the controller and we act as a processor on your instructions (GDPR Art. 28). Atlassian is the hosting sub-processor. We process the minimum needed to provide the app's features, based on our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) and your legitimate interest in running your projects (Art. 6(1)(f)).

4. Storage, location and security

  • Data sits in Atlassian Forge storage, which is encrypted and isolated per installation. It follows your site's Atlassian data residency settings where Forge supports them.
  • We have no copy of your data and no access to it outside the app's own functions running on Atlassian.
  • App permissions are read-only for Jira data (see the app's documentation for the exact scopes).

5. Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept while the app is installed.
  • When you uninstall the app, Atlassian deletes its Forge storage for your site under Atlassian's Forge data lifecycle.
  • Atlassian account IDs are handled under Atlassian's personal data reporting process. If Atlassian reports an account as closed, we remove or anonymise it.

6. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of personal data, and you can object to processing. Because data stays on your Atlassian site, most requests are fastest through your Jira administrator. You can also email andre.aa.johansen@gtmfm.no and we will reply within 30 days. You may complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) or your local authority.

7. Support emails

If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to answer you. We keep support correspondence for up to 24 months, then delete it. Please do not send us personal data or screenshots that contain it unless needed.

8. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.

9. Atlassian

Atlassian's own handling of data is covered by the Atlassian Privacy Policy.

10. Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date. Material changes will be announced on the app's Marketplace listing.
