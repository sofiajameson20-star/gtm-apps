Sprint Scope Tracker for Jira

See every issue that sneaks into, or drops out of, a sprint after it starts. Who did it, and when.

Scope creep hides in plain sight. Jira's sprint report shows an asterisk, but you can't search for those issues, filter them, or see who moved them. Sprint Scope Tracker fixes that.

What you get

  • Two JQL functions. issue in addedAfterSprintStart(42) and issue in removedAfterSprintStart("Team A Sprint 7"). Use them in filters, dashboards, board quick filters and automation, and combine them with any other JQL.
  • A scope-change report on every Jira project. Pick a board and sprint to see how many issues were added and removed, a per-person breakdown, and a timeline of each change with the issue, its status, who made the change and when.
  • History from day one. On install the app rebuilds the last 10 sprints of every Scrum board from Jira's issue history, then tracks new changes live.

Built for trust

  • Runs on Atlassian. Built on Forge. Your data never leaves Atlassian's cloud, and the app calls no outside servers.
  • Read-only. The app never edits your issues or sprints.
  • Respects permissions. People only see changes to issues they can already see in Jira.
